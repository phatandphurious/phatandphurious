Live Report · 3 October 2026 · Le Barri, Montpellier · 5 min read

PnP x Le Barri,
the bedroom moves out.

Six sets filmed in a single afternoon, in a Montpellier neighbourhood bar. Onyx, Klemson, brbr_coeur, Nyctipolos, Ymka and Phat in the same room on the same day, in front of three cameras. The story of a first outing, thunderstorm included.

By Phat · Photos: Phat & Phurious FM and Lolita

The closing set, first of the six to come out. The other five follow over the next few weeks.

6 sets

filmed in one day

6 hours

from 1pm to 7pm

3 cameras

one shoot, no control room

Leaving the bedroom

Since February the principle has fitted into one sentence: one artist, one hour, two cameras, in a bedroom in Montpellier. Seven episodes so far, seven selections, basses hanging on the wall and LEDs tuned to the guest's mood. The format works precisely because it is small, and because you can hear the person behind the decks breathing.

On 3 October we took the whole thing apart and rebuilt it somewhere else. Destination Le Barri, a neighbourhood bar in Montpellier, for a full afternoon. Five of the artists who had already appeared on the show came back for it, plus the house set to close. No ticketing, no guest list, no invitation to beg for: you push the door, that is all.

The homemade sign and the room at Le Barri - PnP x Le Barri, 3 October 2026 · Photos: Phat & Phurious FM

The running order

1:00 pm Onyx Liquid / Drum and Bass
1:51 pm Klemson UK Garage / Hard Bounce
2:42 pm brbr_coeur Breakbeat / Halfstep
3:57 pm Nyctipolos Dubstep
4:48 pm Ymka Bass House / Speed Garage
6:00 pm Phat Bass House / Speed Garage / UK Garage

Onyx opened at one in the afternoon, which amounts to playing liquid drum and bass at coffee time. It was a thankless slot and he played it as a warm-up, wide pads and round breaks, enough to settle the room without shaking it. Klemson followed and pushed it up a notch with his sturdy UK garage, the kind that drifts into hard bounce as soon as it is given room.

brbr_coeur closed the first half with breakbeat that refuses to behave and halfstep that slows the grid without dropping the tempo. Thirty minutes of break after that, long enough to let the gear cool and the glasses fill. Back on, Nyctipolos put dubstep back in the room, then Ymka rolled out an hour of bass house and speed garage that tipped the afternoon firmly into club territory.

Behind and in front of the decks - PnP x Le Barri, 3 October 2026 · Photos: Phat & Phurious FM

A storm outside, a camera overheating inside

Two hitches worth reporting, because a live report without hitches is a live report that lies. The first came from the sky: a heavy thunderstorm over Montpellier in the middle of the afternoon, the kind that discourages anyone still hesitating to go out. The second was internal. The main camera overheated after a few hours of continuous 4K, which is what happens when you ask a stills body to behave like a broadcast rig.

Nothing broken, nothing lost. All six sets are in the can, the sound is clean, and the rest of the day ran exactly as planned on paper. For a first outing of the format, we would have signed for that straight away.

One of the three cameras, in 4K and running continuously - PnP x Le Barri · Photo: Phat & Phurious FM

Le Barri, the other half of the project

A bar willing to block out its afternoon, cancel its own programming and let a crew plant three tripods in the middle of the room is not something you find by snapping your fingers. Le Barri is family. The staff looked after us from start to finish, with no one checking the clock over our shoulder.

Special mention to Pierrot, one of the bar's partners and managers, for trusting a project that had never left a bedroom before. The venue's own decor did the rest: hand-painted t-shirts hanging on a washing line, letter by letter, B A R R I, and a red backdrop that sat perfectly under the LEDs.

Thanks to the six DJs for blocking out their Saturday to play in front of cameras, to Lolita for the photos and the light box sign, and to Alex Chazel, the brother who is always around when something needs carrying, plugging in or holding up. Above all, thanks to everyone who pushed the door open that day: a shoot with nobody in front of it is worth nothing.

What happens next

The closing set went online today, and it is the first of the six. The other five will come out on the channel one after another, not necessarily in the order they were played: each one lands when its edit is ready, and the dates are still to be announced. Subscribing remains the easiest way not to miss them.

One thing is settled: the format knows how to leave the bedroom. Where it goes next is the open question.
